## Description

Rebalances the lamp poles from Power Poles Extended. They take an unnecessary 100 W, which this brings down to a more manageable 30 W, and their light now reaches a little further.

* **Modern power pole with lamp** - 100 W to 30 W, light radius 8 to 13
* **Power pole with lamp** - 100 W to 30 W, light radius 5 to 13

## Installation

Subscribe on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3650142147), or copy the `Mod` folder from this mod's folder in the repository into your RimWorld `Mods` folder and rename it to `power-poles-electricity-rebalance`.

**REMOVE ANY OLD VERSIONS BEFORE INSTALLING**.

## Compatibility

Requires [Power Poles Extended](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3339142899), and therefore [Power Poles](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2507086460). Works with anything else that doesn't change the same poles.
