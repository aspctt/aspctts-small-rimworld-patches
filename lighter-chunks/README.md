## Description

Divides the mass of every stone chunk by five, so they stop eating up caravan and transport pod space.

* **Sandstone** - 20 kg to 4 kg
* **Granite** - 25 kg to 5 kg
* **Limestone** - 22 kg to 4.4 kg
* **Slate** - 18 kg to 3.6 kg
* **Marble** - 25 kg to 5 kg
* **Vacstone** - 18 kg to 3.6 kg, with Odyssey on RimWorld 1.6

## Installation

Subscribe on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3650156682), or copy the `Mod` folder from this mod's folder in the repository into your RimWorld `Mods` folder and rename it to `lighter-chunks`.

**REMOVE ANY OLD VERSIONS BEFORE INSTALLING**.

## Compatibility

No dependencies. Works with anything that doesn't change the same chunks.
